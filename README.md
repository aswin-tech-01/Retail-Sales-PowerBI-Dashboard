# Retail Sales Power BI Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

An interactive Retail Sales Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales, profit, customers, products, regions, cities, categories, and sales channels.

The dashboard provides an overview of business performance along with detailed time-series analysis.

## 📌 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Overview

The Overview page provides a high-level view of retail business performance.

**KPIs:**
- Total Sales
- Total Customers
- Total Orders
- Total Products
- Total Profit

**Analysis:**
- Sales by Region
- Sales by Brand & City
- Top Products by Sales
- Profit by Region
- Category Performance
- Sales by Channel

### 2. Time Series Analysis

The Time Series Analysis page focuses on sales and business performance over time.

**Analysis:**
- Monthly Sales Trend
- 3-Month Moving Average
- Monthly Sales by Year
- Monthly Profit by Year
- Monthly Sales by Region
- Monthly Orders by Year
- Monthly Sales by Channel

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to transform retail sales data into an interactive business intelligence dashboard that helps users understand sales and profitability trends and compare performance across different regions, products, cities, categories, and channels.
